Adaptations inherited from Human Atlas: axes and units converted from millimeters/Z-up to meters/Y-up; translated to rest at the stage; geometry simplified with a 0.2% relative error limit per structure; normals quantized to signed 16-bit; packed into binary chunks; curated display-system groupings and colors. All 2,234 source meshes remain represented in the manifest.

BodyParts3D represents an adult male reference anatomy. It does not represent every human structure or variation. Parthia uses it only to provide educational body-system context for medication warnings; it is not a patient-specific model, diagnosis, or surgical tool.
